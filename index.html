About

I am a second-year mathematics PhD student in Paris at École Polytechnique and Université Paris-Saclay, advised by Michael Goldman and Cyril Letrouit.

My PhD research is on quantitative stability of the optimal transport problem. I have written two preprints on this subject; see Research.

I am also actively working on mathematical aspects of AI safety and alignment, as well as autoformalisation of mathematics in Lean.

Contact: william.ford@polytechnique.edu. Offices: 2A1 at LMO and 2008 at CMAP.
